[02] About HLF

Rooted in tradition. Alive with possibility.

A meeting of minds. A celebration of the Prophetic tradition.

HLF is an academic and cultural initiative bringing Hadith literature into conversation with thought, expression and creativity.

Organized by the Department of Hadith and Related Sciences at Darul Huda Islamic University, the festival celebrates the rich legacy of the Prophetic tradition and its scholarly heritage.

Through lectures, panel discussions, exhibitions and interactive sessions, we explore how Hadith has been preserved, transmitted and understood — and why it continues to matter today.

For students, researchers, scholars and curious minds across campuses.
